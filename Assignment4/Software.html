As an Artist, I have used a multitude of different softwares in my time for digital art. I wanted to compare two of the primary softwares I have used. Clip Studio Paint, and Photoshop.

Name Cost Animation Support UI Customization Image Editing Software Stability Begginer Friendly
CLIP STUDIO PAINT One time $63 Purchase Limited Basic Animation Support Customizable Color Wheel, Toolbars, Layer Areas, and Brushes. Limited Image editing capabilities. Stable and Smooth Software. Takes A Few Months To Adjust To Using.
PHOTOSHOP $22.99 Monthly Charge Clunky/Difficult To Use Animation Timeline Customizable Color Wheel and Toolbar. Multitude Of Different Image Editing Options and Tools. Software Can Be Prone To Crashing On Larger Files. Able To Pick Up and Learn Within Days.

I cannot definitively say that one software is objectively better than the other. However I can compare the two based on my own personal experiences, and compare and contrast the things I look for in a drawing software.
