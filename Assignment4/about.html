About My Art

I have been teaching myself art for the past 3 years. I started my journey on physical paper, doodling on school notebooks and scrap paper. I eventually moved to photoshop, using skills I learned from my high school classes to improve from there. I eventually moved to Clip Studio Paint. I have taught myself how to draw through years of trial and error. And thankfully there is still plenty of room and time to grow.
